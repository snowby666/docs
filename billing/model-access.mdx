Understanding which models you can access and how they're billed.


## Model Categories

Electron Hub has three main model categories:

<Accordion title="Free Models (:free suffix)" icon="gift">
  <Card title="Free Models" icon="gift" color="#10B981">
    - Models that end with `:free` (e.g., `gpt-3.5-turbo:free`)
    - Use **Neutrinos** instead of credits
    - No subscription required
    - Best for: Testing, light usage, learning
  </Card>
  
  <Warning>
    <strong>Uptime:</strong> Not guaranteed. Free models are served by third-party providers on shared, limited capacity, so requests may be queued, slowed or refused at peak times.
  </Warning>

  <Warning>
    <strong>Privacy:</strong> Providers of free models may use the prompts you send them to train their models. Don't send anything sensitive to a `:free` model.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Freemium Models" icon="star">
  <Card title="Freemium Models" icon="star" color="#8B5CF6">
    - Models that don't require a subscription
    - **Do deduct credits** from your balance
    - Available to all users (Free tier and above)
    - Better uptime than free models
    - Best for: Regular usage without subscription commitment
  </Card>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Pro and Ultimate Models" icon="crown">
  <Card title="Pro Models" icon="crown" color="#F59E0B">
    - Models that **need a Starter plan or above**, or **pay-as-you-go credits** on a free account
    - Deduct credits from your balance
    - Better uptime than free models
    - Marked `premium_model: true` in [`/v1/models`](/api-reference/models)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ultimate Models" icon="infinity" color="#EF4444">
    - The top frontier models: **need the Pro plan or above** (Pro, Business, Enterprise)
    - Deduct credits from your balance
    - Marked `ultimate_model: true` in [`/v1/models`](/api-reference/models) (they are also Pro models)
  </Card>
</Accordion>

## Model Access by Plan

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="gift" color="#10B981">
    - Free models (with Neutrinos)
    - Freemium models (with credits)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Free + pay-as-you-go credits" icon="wallet" color="#8B5CF6">
    - Free models
    - Freemium models
    - **Pro models**
  </Card>

  <Card title="Starter / Plus / Core" icon="star" color="#F59E0B">
    - Free and freemium models
    - Pro models
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pro / Business / Enterprise" icon="infinity" color="#EF4444">
    - Every model, including
    - Pro models
    - **Ultimate models**
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
  <strong>Note:</strong> "Pro models" is a group of models, not the Pro plan: any paid plan from Starter up (or pay-as-you-go credits) unlocks them. Ultimate models need the Pro plan or above. The app and the [model list](https://www.electronhub.ai/models) badge both groups.
</Info>

## Why don't free models guarantee 100% uptime?

  <Info>
    Free models are served by third-party providers on shared, limited capacity. During peak times requests may be queued, slowed or refused, and paid plans may be served first.
  </Info>

<Tip>
  For better uptime and performance, we recommend:
  - Using freemium models (deduct credits but better uptime)
  - Subscribing to a paid plan to use Pro models (routed to multiple providers)
</Tip>
