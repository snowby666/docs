This page summarises how Electron Hub handles your data. The [Privacy Policy](https://tos.electronhub.ai/privacy) is the full, binding version; all of our policies are in the [Legal Center](https://tos.electronhub.ai/legal).

## What We Keep

<Info>
  **We don't keep a copy of your conversations.** For each request we log metadata only:
  - Request metadata (model used, tokens, credits, timestamp, status)
  - Technical information (IP address, API key used, route accessed)
  - Usage statistics (for billing and analytics)

  Request logs are kept for **30 days**, up to the latest **1,000** requests per account. They are what you see in [User Logs](/api-reference/user-logs).
</Info>

<Note>
  **When content is kept:** only in the limited cases described in [section 5 of the Privacy Policy](https://tos.electronhub.ai/privacy#content):
  - **Router cache**, if you turn it on: responses are kept for the period you choose (1 minute to 30 days), for your account only
  - **Error diagnostics**: when a request fails with an error other than a rate limit, the diagnostic record can include the request content
  - **Coding Plan**: short samples of recent requests per `ek-dev-` key, used for fair-use enforcement
  - **Image generation**: free-plan generations and requests sent with `public: true` are added to the gallery
  - **Abuse cases**: content linked to a suspected violation, an abuse investigation or a legal report
</Note>

## Model Providers

Your requests are sent to the model provider that serves the model you choose, and that provider processes them under its own terms. Some providers keep data for a period or review it for abuse.

<Warning>
  **Free models:** `:free` and experimental models are served by third-party providers who may use your prompts and outputs to train their models. Don't send anything sensitive to them.
</Warning>

Electron Hub does not train models on your prompts or outputs.

## Content Moderation

Image and video generation prompts (including images you ask for in chat) and images submitted for editing are screened before generation. They are checked against our [Usage Policy](https://tos.electronhub.ai/usage-policy), which prohibits, among other things:

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="CSAM Detection" icon="ban" color="#EF4444">
    **CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material)**: Any content related to child exploitation
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Illegal Activities" icon="ban" color="#F59E0B">
    Content about making drugs, bombs, weapons, or other illegal activities
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Harmful Content" icon="stop-circle" color="#EF4444">
    Content that could cause real-world harm
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
  **How it works:**
  - Image and video prompts are checked automatically before generation, using automated classifiers, some run by third-party moderation providers
  - Flagged requests are blocked with a `403` and nothing is generated
  - No credits are charged for blocked requests
  - Chat text is not screened before it reaches the model; model providers apply their own policies
</Info>

<Note>
  **Review:**
  - Blocking is automated. Content linked to a suspected violation, an abuse investigation or a legal report may be kept and reviewed by our team, and suspected CSAM is reported to the authorities as the law requires
  - To ask for a person to review an automated decision, email support@electronhub.ai with the subject "Review"
</Note>

## Security

Traffic to Electron Hub is encrypted in transit (TLS). We don't offer end-to-end encryption: requests are decrypted on our servers so they can be routed to the model provider. See [Security](https://tos.electronhub.ai/security) for details and [Subprocessors](https://tos.electronhub.ai/subprocessors) for the services we use.
