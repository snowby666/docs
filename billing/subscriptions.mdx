Subscriptions provide weekly credits that automatically refill every Sunday, along with access to Pro features and models.

<Note>
  **Changing on January 3, 2027 at 21:00 UTC**

  - **Lower model prices, up to 70% below OpenRouter.** Every model shows OpenRouter's price next to ours; discounts vary by model. For example, per 1M tokens (input / output): Gemini 3.1 Pro $0.60 / $3.60 (OpenRouter $2 / $12), DeepSeek V4 Pro $0.38 / $0.77 (OpenRouter $1.28 / $2.56), Kimi K2.6 $0.20 / $1.02 (OpenRouter $0.65 / $3.41).
  - **Weekly credits, sized for the new prices:** Starter $3 (was $8), Plus $6 (was $15), Core $12 (was $30), Pro $20 (was $50), Business $40 (was $100), Enterprise $75 (was $200). On a model at half its OpenRouter price, the new amount buys about what double it buys today. Plan prices don't change.
  - **Pay-as-you-go:** $1 buys 1 credit at the new prices. Credits you bought before November 24, 2026 stay exactly as they are. Credits bought from November 24 are converted to the new rate on January 3, so they keep the same buying power; checkout shows this before you pay.
  - **Flex wallet:** save up to 10% of your weekly credits each week, and hold up to 25%.
  - **Not changing:** the Free plan's $0.25 a week, Neutrinos, rate limits, the Coding Plan and the Sunday refill.

  Until January 3, everything on this page describes how credits work today.
</Note>

## Subscription Tiers

Electron Hub offers several monthly subscription tiers, available through **Creem** (recommended), **lava.top**, or **Ko-fi**:

| Tier | Weekly Credits | Max Bots | Studio Credits |
|------|----------------|----------|----------------|
| Free | $0.25 | 5 | 5 |
| Starter | $8 | 20 | 20 |
| Plus | $15 | 30 | 50 |
| Core | $30 | 50 | 75 |
| Pro | $50 | 100 | 150 |
| Business | $100 | 150 | 300 |
| Enterprise | $200 | 200 | 500 |

<Card title="Benefits of Higher Tiers" icon="arrow-up" color="#8B5CF6">
  - More weekly credits
  - Access to Pro models (and Ultimate models on Pro and above)
  - Higher rate limits
  - More bot creation slots
  - More studio credits (for AI bot creation)
</Card>

## Annual vs Monthly Billing

<Card title="Save 20% with Annual Billing" icon="percent" color="#10B981">
  **Annual subscriptions save you 20%** compared to monthly billing!
  
  **Example (Creem Pro):**
  - Monthly Pro tier: `$69`/month
  - Annual Pro tier: `$69` × 12 × 0.8 = `$662.40`/year (Save 20%)
</Card>

<Tip>
  Annual subscriptions are perfect for users who:
  - Plan to use the service long-term
  - Want to maximize value
  - Prefer fewer payment transactions
</Tip>

## How to Subscribe

You can subscribe with **Creem** (recommended), **lava.top**, or **Ko-fi**. All three grant the same tier benefits. You can only have one active subscription at a time.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Creem (Recommended)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Billing">
        Go to the [Electron Hub Dashboard](https://app.electronhub.ai) → **Console** → **Billing** → **Subscription**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your plan">
        Pick a tier and billing period (monthly or annual), then click **Subscribe**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete checkout">
        You'll be redirected to Creem's hosted checkout to enter your payment details.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Activate Benefits">
        Once Creem confirms the payment (usually within a few seconds), your subscription benefits are applied automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      Creem subscribers get a **Manage billing** button (update card, invoices, change plan) and a **Cancel** option on the Billing → Subscription page.
    </Info>

    <Tip>
      Creem pricing is slightly higher than Ko-fi's for the same tier, to cover payment processing fees. In-app benefits are identical either way.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="lava.top">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Billing">
        Go to the [Electron Hub Dashboard](https://app.electronhub.ai) → **Console** → **Billing** → **Subscription**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose plan and currency">
        Pick a tier, billing period (monthly or annual), and currency (**USD**, **EUR**, or **RUB**), then start checkout.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete checkout">
        You'll be redirected to lava.top to finish payment.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Activate Benefits">
        Once lava.top confirms the payment (usually within a minute or two), your subscription benefits are applied automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      lava.top has no billing portal and no in-place plan change. Cancel from Billing → Subscription (access continues until the paid period ends). To change plan, cancel first, then subscribe again.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Ko-fi">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Verify your email">
        Go to the [Electron Hub Dashboard](https://app.electronhub.ai) → **Console** → **Billing** → **Subscription** and verify your account email.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Subscribe on Ko-fi">
        Join the monthly membership for your desired tier on the Electron Hub Ko-fi page, using the same email as your verified Electron Hub account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Activate Benefits">
        Once Ko-fi confirms the payment (usually within ~1 minute), it's matched to your account by email and your subscription benefits are applied.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      <strong>Important:</strong> The email you pay with on Ko-fi must match your verified Electron Hub email exactly. If your email isn't verified yet when the payment arrives, it's held as pending and applied once you verify.
    </Warning>

    <Info>
      Ko-fi monthly memberships renew automatically. Ko-fi annual plans are one-time shop purchases (12 months, no auto-renew).
    </Info>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  You can only have one active subscription provider at a time (Creem, lava.top, or Ko-fi). Cancel the existing one before switching. On Creem you can also change plans in the billing portal.
</Warning>

## Subscription Renewal

<Card title="Automatic Renewal" icon="repeat" color="#8B5CF6">
  **Creem**, **lava.top**, and **Ko-fi monthly** memberships renew automatically. **Ko-fi annual** plans are one-time purchases for 12 months and do not auto-renew. Your expiry date is shown on the Billing → Subscription page.
</Card>

## What Happens When Subscription Expires?

<Warning>
  If your subscription expires:
  - You'll be downgraded to the Free tier
  - You'll receive \$0.25 weekly credits
  - You'll lose access to Pro and Ultimate models
  - Your permanent credits remain (they don't expire while your account is open)
  - Your flex credits are removed
</Warning>

## Weekly Credits Refill

<Info>
  Weekly credits refill every **Sunday at 21:00 UTC** (9:00 PM UTC). Each refill tops your balance up to your plan's weekly allowance; unused weekly credits are <strong>not</strong> added on top. To set some aside for a heavier week, save them as [Flex Credits](/billing/credits#flex-credits).
</Info>
