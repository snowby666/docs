Pay-as-You-Go allows you to purchase permanent credits that don't expire while your account is open. This gives you maximum flexibility without any recurring commitment.


## What are Permanent Credits?

<Card icon="wallet" color="#10B981">
  **Permanent Credits** are credits you purchase with real money. Unlike weekly credits, these credits:
  - **Don't expire** while your account is open
  - Can be purchased by **card** (Creem), through **Ko-fi**, or with **cryptocurrency**
  - Can be used alongside weekly credits
  - Have volume discounts (the more you spend, the more bonus credits you get)
</Card>

## Pricing

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Base Rate" icon="calculator" color="#8B5CF6">
    **`$1` USD = 2 credits**
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Volume Discounts" icon="percent" color="#10B981">
    - Every `$500` spent: **5% bonus**
    - Maximum discount: **20% bonus** (at `$2,000+` total spending)
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Card title="Pricing Examples" icon="chart-line" color="#F59E0B">
  - First purchase: `$100` = 200 credits (no bonus yet)
  - After `$500` total: `$100` purchase = 210 credits (5% bonus)
  - After `$2,000` total: `$100` purchase = 240 credits (20% bonus, maximum)
</Card>

## Payment Methods

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Card (Creem)" icon="credit-card" color="#0070BA">
    - **Range:** `$5` - `$5,000` USD
    - **Methods:** Card, Google Pay, Apple Pay, Alipay
    - **Processing:** Usually a few seconds
    - **Credits:** Added once Creem confirms the payment
    
    <Info>
      <strong>Best for:</strong> Quick purchases, users who prefer traditional payment methods
    </Info>
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ko-fi" icon="mug-hot" color="#13C3FF">
    - **Methods:** PayPal or card, on Ko-fi
    - **Currency:** USD (payments in other currencies are held for manual review)
    - **Matching:** Put the `KF-` link code from the dashboard in your Ko-fi message, or pay with your account email
    - **Credits:** Added once Ko-fi confirms the payment
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Cryptocurrency" icon="bitcoin" color="#F7931A">
    - **Range:** `$25` - `$1,000` USD
    - **Fees:** 5% processing fee
    - **Processing:** 10 minutes to 24 hours
    - **Credits:** Added after confirmation
    - **Supported:** 300+ cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, USDT, LTC, DOGE, SOL, and more)
    
    <Warning>
      Orders expire after 24 hours if payment is not confirmed.
    </Warning>
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How to Purchase

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Dashboard">
    Go to the [Electron Hub Dashboard](https://app.electronhub.ai)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Payment">
    Navigate to the credits/payment section
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Payment Method">
    Choose **Card** (Creem), **Ko-fi** or **Cryptocurrency**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Amount">
    Select the amount you want to purchase (see Payment Methods above for limits)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Complete payment through your chosen method
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credits Added">
    Credits are added to your account once the payment is confirmed (card: usually seconds, crypto: 10 minutes to 24 hours)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Limits

<Card title="Purchase Limits" icon="lock" color="#EF4444">
  - **Daily limit**: 20 orders per day
  - **Monthly limit**: `$5,000` USD per month
  - **Transaction limits**: See Payment Methods section above
  - Limits reset automatically
</Card>

## Monthly Spending Limits

<Card title="Spending Controls" icon="sliders" color="#8B5CF6">
  You can set a **monthly spending limit** for permanent credits to control costs:
  - **0 = Unlimited** (no limit)
  - **Any positive number** = Maximum amount you can spend per month
  - **Maximum limit**: `$100,000` USD per month
  - Limits reset on the 1st of each month
</Card>

<Tip>
  <strong>Use cases:</strong>
  - Budget control for teams
  - Preventing accidental overspending
  - Managing costs for production applications
</Tip>

## Rate Limit Scaling

<Info>
  Your rate limits scale based on your **total purchased credits** (permanent credits spending). See [Rate Limit Scaling](/billing/advanced#rate-limit-scaling) for complete details.
</Info>

## Low Balance Alerts

<Card title="Stay Informed" icon="envelope" color="#F59E0B">
  You can enable **low balance alerts** for permanent credits:
  - Set a threshold (e.g., 10 credits)
  - Receive email alerts when your permanent credits balance drops below the threshold
  - Alerts are sent at most once every 24 hours (to prevent spam)
</Card>

## Refund Policy

<Info>
  <strong>Permanent Credits Purchases:</strong> You can ask for a refund of unused permanent credits within 14 days of purchase; bonus credits and checkout payment fees are deducted. Credits you have already used and crypto payments are not refundable, except where the law requires. For complete terms, see our [Refund & Billing Policy](https://tos.electronhub.ai/refunds) and [Refund Requests](/billing/troubleshooting#refund-requests).
</Info>
